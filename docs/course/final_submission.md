# Final submission requirements

(Verbatim summary from `prompts/campaign.md`, appendix.) Due **Tue Dec 15 23:59**; hard cutoff Wed Dec 16 17:00;
100 pts.

1. **Final report, 50 pts.** LaTeX conference format (IEEE, arXiv, CVPR, ICRA, IROS, CoRL…), compiled PDF, written
   like a real research paper; all diagrams and plots vector graphics. Structure: title, authors, clickable links
   to repo and video; abstract 150–250 words with key quantitative results; 1 Introduction (application,
   importance, limits of existing solutions, bulleted contributions); 2 Related Work; 3 Methodology / System
   Architecture (pipeline, architecture, math/losses, dataset preparation, design choices, vector architecture
   diagram); 4 Experiments & Ablations (hardware/software setup, metrics, baselines, latency/memory profiling,
   qualitative visualizations, ablations); 5 Failure Analysis, Negative Results & Limitations (concrete examples,
   root-cause diagnosis); 6 Conclusion & Future Work; References.
2. **GitHub repo, 30 pts.** Public; clean README with structure, environment setup, how to run
   training/evaluation/inference, links to checkpoints or datasets; clear meaningful commit history.
3. **Video demo, 20 pts.** Strictly ≤ 2 minutes, creative and engaging from the first second, shows the working
   demo and visual results; YouTube (public/unlisted) or Google Drive link.
