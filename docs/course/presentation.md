# Presentation requirements

(Verbatim summary from `prompts/campaign.md`, appendix.)

Dec 1/3; Aaron presents **Thu Dec 3**; **10 min**; slides .pptx or .pdf uploaded after the talk (due Dec 3,
closes Dec 10). Tell a clear, visual, logically cohesive engineering story; no text-heavy slides or raw output dumps.

Flow:
1. Application & problem context: target application, operational scenario and constraints (latency, hardware,
   domain shift, safety), the exact problem.
2. Existing technical landscape & baselines: how SOTA/open-source baselines approach it and what gaps remain.
3. What we did: pipeline, model choices, data curation, modifications, and why each design decision.
4. Experiments & ablations (core): controlled quantitative evaluations, latency/resource trade-offs, qualitative
   visual comparisons, ablations isolating components.
5. Key insights & negative results: what failed, why, how it was diagnosed.
